Source of truth · UI never invents status

Four layered lifecycles

A request becomes one booking; a booking has one job; a job has one or more visits. Each has its own enum. Chip = friendly label, enum underneath. A dispute is a hold, opened inside the 24 h confirm window — not a status of these lifecycles.

Old merged lifecycle → replaced

Requested → Confirmed → On the way → Arrived → In progress → Awaiting payment → Completed → Disputed

“On the way”, “Arrived”, “In progress” are visit states. “Awaiting payment” is not a status — it is a non-zero dueMinor on the bill. “Disputed” is a hold on a visit, job or cash payment (LLD-018), shown as a banner plus the dispute chip.
