Session states (LLD-002 §4.3, D2, D4)One screen per cause, never "log in with OTP":
• C-06a REFRESH_TOKEN_INVALID (idle 30 days / login older than 90 days). 401 TOKEN_EXPIRED alone is silent — the app refreshes once and retries.
• C-06b SESSION_REVOKED / REFRESH_TOKEN_REUSED (logout-all, password change, device removed, reuse detected).
• C-06c 403 ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED on login or refresh. The reason is never shown on screen or in push (LLD-013 D5, D12) — it goes by email. Support = phone / WhatsApp link (no chat, no call-back, P1/P19). Support number is a placeholder.
